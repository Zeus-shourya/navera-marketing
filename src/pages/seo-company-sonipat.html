How it works

How Google picks the businesses people see nearby

When someone in Sonipat searches “salon near me”, “café in Sonipat” or “jewellery showroom Sonipat”, Google shows a map with a handful of businesses before any website. Google says it ranks these local results mainly on three things:

  • Relevance — how well your profile matches what the person searched for.
  • Distance — how close your business is to the searcher or the place in their search.
  • Prominence — how well known and trusted your business is, including reviews and your presence across the web.

You can’t move your shop closer to every customer, but relevance and prominence are in your hands. That is exactly what our SEO and Google Business Profile work improves.
