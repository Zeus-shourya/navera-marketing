How it works

From first chat to first campaign

A simple workflow keeps the work clear, collaborative and moving.

  1. Discover

    We understand your business, audience, goals and current digital presence.

  2. Plan

    We agree on the scope, content pillars, monthly calendar and approval process.

  3. Create

    We produce the agreed videos, graphics, copy and campaign assets.

  4. Publish & manage

    Content is scheduled or published and campaigns are monitored within the agreed scope.

  5. Review

    You get a summary of completed work, key metrics and recommended next steps.
