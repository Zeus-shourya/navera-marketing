FAQ

About our website work

Are these real, live websites?

Yes. Every project on this page is live on the internet right now: tap any card to open it in a new tab and try it on your phone. Most are client websites. The ones marked Concept demo or Web app are working builds we made to show what is possible, such as interactive 3D sales tools for builders.

How much does a website like these cost?

Our website packages are Basic at ₹6,999, Catalogue at ₹14,999 (hosting included) and E-commerce at ₹29,999 (hosting and domain included). Interactive plot maps, 3D tools and other custom features are quoted after a short conversation about what you need.

All prices are negotiable, and GST is extra if applicable.

Can I update my website myself?

Yes, where it helps. Sites such as Aggarwal Properties are connected to a simple online editor, so the owner marks plots as sold or available without touching any code. We can set up the same for your listings, products, prices, photos or contact details.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every website we build includes what Google looks for: page titles and descriptions written for local searches, structured data, a sitemap, and fast, mobile-friendly pages. Rankings also depend on competition and on your Google Business Profile, so we never promise the #1 spot, but we set your site up to compete for it.

Do you only make websites for businesses in Sonipat?

We are based in Sonipat, and our clients are in Sonipat, Rohtak, Bahadurgarh, Mohali, Delhi and beyond. A website can be planned, reviewed and delivered over WhatsApp and calls, wherever your business is.
