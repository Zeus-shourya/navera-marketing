FAQ

Questions, answered

How much budget do I need for ads in Sonipat?

It depends on your goal, your offer and how competitive your category is. Many local businesses start with a modest test budget and scale what works. We will recommend a starting budget after understanding your business.

Is the ad spend included in your fee?

No. Advertising spend is paid by you directly to Meta or Google and is separate from our management fee, so you always see exactly what was spent.

Can my ads target only people in Sonipat?

Yes. Both Meta and Google let us target by city, by area around a location, or by specific places, so your budget is spent on people who can actually visit or call you.

How soon will ads bring enquiries?

Campaigns can start delivering as soon as they are approved by the platform, but performance improves with testing and optimisation. Results depend on your market, offer, budget and customer behaviour, so we never promise a fixed number of leads.
