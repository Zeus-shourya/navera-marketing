FAQ

Digital marketing in Sonipat, answered

Which is the best digital marketing agency in Sonipat?

The best agency for you is one that understands your customers, shows you clear plans and prices, and can create content on location in Sonipat. Navera Marketing is a Sonipat-based creative and digital team: we plan, shoot, design and publish for you, and every package has a written scope.

The easiest way to judge us is to message us on WhatsApp about your business. We'll suggest a practical plan and confirm the scope before any work begins.

What digital marketing services does Navera offer?

Six services under one roof: social media management, reels and video production, graphic design and branding, website development, paid advertising on Meta and Google, and SEO with Google Business Profile support.

How much does digital marketing cost in Sonipat?

Our monthly growth plans are Essential at ₹9,999, Business Growth at ₹19,999 and Premium Growth at ₹24,999 per month. Websites are a one-time fee: Basic ₹6,999, Catalogue ₹14,999 and E-commerce ₹29,999.

Prices are service fees in INR; GST is extra if applicable, and ad spend is paid directly to Meta or Google. All prices are negotiable, and every package can be customised after a discovery conversation.

Do you shoot reels at our shop, showroom or office?

Yes. Planned content shoots are built into every monthly plan: 2 shoots a month on Essential, 4 on Business Growth, and shoots planned around your reel volume on Premium Growth. We bring camera, lighting, audio and stabilisation gear suited to the brief and location.

Is ad spend included in your packages?

No. Advertising budget is paid by you directly to Meta or Google, so you always see exactly what was spent. Our fee covers planning, setup, creatives, monitoring and reporting within the agreed scope.

Can you guarantee followers, leads or sales?

No honest agency can. Results depend on your market, offer, budget and customer behaviour. What we do promise is agreed deliverables, consistent quality and a monthly summary of the work, key metrics and next steps.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Sonipat and work with businesses across Sonipat city and the district, including Kundli, Murthal, Rai, Ganaur, Gohana and Kharkhoda. Websites, ads and SEO can be handled online for brands anywhere in India.
