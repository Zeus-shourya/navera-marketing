Why it matters

People in Sonipat check Instagram first

Before someone visits a new café, books a salon, walks into a jewellery showroom or calls about a property, they usually look you up on Instagram. An inactive page or a random mix of posts quietly costs you that customer.

A consistent page with fresh reels and stories does the opposite: it shows you are open, active and worth the trip. That is what our social media management is built for — a premium, recognisable feed and a steady rhythm of content that local customers actually stop to watch.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How many reels and posts does my business need each month?

Consistency matters more than volume. Our Essential plan covers 10 reels and 10 story creatives a month, Business Growth covers 16 reels, 16 stories and 8 graphic creatives, and Premium Growth sets reel volume around your requirements with 20 stories and 12 creatives.

Do you shoot the content, or do we?

We do. Planned content shoots are included in every plan (2 a month on Essential, 4 on Business Growth, and planned around your reel volume on Premium Growth). You are always welcome to share your own photos and clips too.

Which platforms do you manage?

Our monthly plans cover Instagram and Facebook management. If you need other platforms, tell us during the discovery conversation and we will confirm the scope in your service agreement.

Will I approve posts before they go live?

Yes. We agree the monthly calendar with you, and your approval is required for facts, prices, offers and claims before anything is published.

Can you also run Instagram and Facebook ads?

Yes, through our paid advertising service. Ad spend is paid by you directly to Meta and is separate from our management fee.
