FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you shoot at our location in Sonipat?

Yes. We shoot on location at your shop, showroom, restaurant, site or office. Travel outside the agreed area, studio rental and specialist production needs can affect the final quote.

How many shoots are included in a monthly plan?

Essential includes 2 planned content shoots a month, Business Growth includes 4, and on Premium Growth shoots are planned around the number of reels you need.

Who comes up with the reel ideas?

We do. Reel concepts and short-form storytelling are part of the service: we plan hooks, shots and edits around your products, services and audience, and agree them with you before the shoot.

What equipment do you use?

A quality camera setup with suitable lenses, portable lighting and modifiers, wireless microphones and audio accessories, and tripods, mounts and stabilisers where the shoot needs them. We plan the setup around each brief.

Are models, influencers or talent included?

No. Influencer fees, talent, and major production expenses are separate unless they are written into your final quote.
