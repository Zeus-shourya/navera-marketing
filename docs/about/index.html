Our story

Great local businesses deserve great brands

Sonipat is full of businesses that are excellent at what they do: jewellers with decades of craft, cafés and dhabas people drive in for, salons, showrooms, clinics and builders with real reputations. Too often, their online presence doesn’t show any of that.

Navera Marketing exists to close that gap. We bring the strategy, production and design of a big-city agency to businesses in Sonipat, and we stay close enough to visit your space, shoot on location and understand your customers.

We are one creative partner for every moving part of your digital presence: social media, reels and video, graphic design, websites, paid ads and Google visibility — working together instead of across five different vendors.

  • Ideas → impact

    Every reel, post and page has a job to do for your business.

  • Scroll-stopping, always on-brand

    Premium creative that still looks and sounds like you.

  • Clear scope

    Agreed deliverables, a monthly calendar and your approval before anything is published.

  • Honest numbers

    We report on the work and the metrics, and never promise guaranteed sales or virality.

How it works

From first chat to first campaign

A simple workflow keeps the work clear, collaborative and moving.

  2. Plan
